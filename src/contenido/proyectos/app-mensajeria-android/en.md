This was my final project for my software development degree (DAM). It's an Android messaging app with end-to-end encryption, image sharing and events inside each conversation: with a location on the map, alarms, and synced with the other person. It also has privacy controls: real-time activity status, a "typing" indicator and control over what personal information each contact can see.

I built it in Java, with Firebase, and wrote every interface in XML by hand, adjusted to the pixel. No AI: six months of tutorials, documentation and Stack Overflow until everything worked.
