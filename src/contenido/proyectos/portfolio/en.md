I'd had the idea of making a portfolio for years, but I had neither the experience nor the projects to show. Now I have them, and even though I have less time than ever, I can build it faster without losing control of every detail. I wanted a site that made one thing clear: I like what I do, and I like doing it well.

The hardest part was defining the design. It follows my brand, Vørlys: black and white, everything at weight 400, and motion only where it's earned.

The split was clear. The design and the copy are mine. The code was written by Claude Code following my programming guidelines, so it's easy to read and easy to debug, and I reviewed that everything was right. It's built with Vue 3, Vite and Tailwind, and generated as static pages so it loads fast on any phone and can be read even without JavaScript.
